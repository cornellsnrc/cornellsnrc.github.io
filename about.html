About SNRC

A broad community for the natural world.

We connect students who care about conservation—whether their path starts in ecology, engineering, agriculture, policy, or simply outside.

Our purpose

Create welcoming, useful ways for Cornell students to understand natural resources and take part in their stewardship.

How we work

Curiosity becomes participation.

Members can learn, teach, plan, collaborate, or just show up. Every level of involvement is useful.

01

Learn across disciplines

Bring together scientific, technical, cultural, and policy perspectives.

02

Practice in the field

Use local landscapes as places to observe, build skills, and ask better questions.

03

Make room for people

Keep events welcoming to new members and accessible to students from any major.
